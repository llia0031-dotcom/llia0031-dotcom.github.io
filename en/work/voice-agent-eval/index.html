Real-time voice agent evaluationCan a voice agent stay right when interrupted?

Measures a cascaded real-time voice agent (voice activity detection → speech recognition → a model with tools → speech synthesis) on 42 scripted Chinese and English conversations covering pauses, interruptions, backchannels, noise and tool calls.

In one minute

Whether a voice assistant still gets the task right when the user pauses, interrupts, says “mm” or talks over background speech, with experience measured as latency, barge-in and false-stop numbers.

I wrote 42 conversations with pauses, barge-ins, backchannels and noise, answers fixed in advance, and built a local cascaded voice agent as the baseline; then tried fixes one at a time, each with its own preregistration addendum, and combined the ones that worked.

The four fixes together are the best setting tested; the remaining backchannel problem cannot be solved from transcribed text and needs the audio itself.

What I assumed

Expected a fixed recognition language to tell “mm” from a barge-in

What the data showed

“Mm” was still heard as other words; text alone cannot tell, the audio must

  • 4.56 s Wait before replying over background talk (was 20.4 s) median, n=8, 95% CI 2.97 to 5.69
  • 0.25 s How fast it stops when the user cuts in median, n=16, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.28
  • 16/16 Stopped every time the user really cut in 100.0%, 95% CI 80.6 to 100.0%

Limits

01User speech is synthesised and time is simulated

with 42 conversations and two runs per setting, a difference of one or two is noise. Recommendation: Before launch, rerun with recorded human speech (for example a public dialogue speech set) and measure latency on a real network.

02Backchannels such as “mm-mm” still interrupt

the recogniser hears other words, and text alone cannot tell a backchannel from a short barge-in. Recommendation: Use detection on the audio itself; an off-the-shelf acoustic backchannel model was tried offline and missed the preset bar, so it was not added.

03No end-to-end real-time speech model was compared

Recommendation: Add an end-to-end model with a free tier and compare it on the same items and scorer.
