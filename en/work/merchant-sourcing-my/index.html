In one minute

A category team’s first week of merchant recruiting: what the market looks like, which merchants to contact first and why, which approvals each product needs, how onboarding is tracked; every reason backed by a public source.

I first checked the existing shop-ranking tools: they rank merchants already on a platform and cannot answer “which merchants not yet there, or not yet large, to recruit first”. This pack fills that gap from public sources: saved official, market and brand pages, an industry insight and a per-category approvals checklist; scoring rules written and frozen before any row was filled, ranking local brands, distributors and retailers; then an onboarding tracker, first-30-days SQL and outreach templates; a one-page deep dive on chargers, power banks and cables; and a guide to which public fields to replace with team data first and how to test the scoring weights against results, with a calibration script. Every fact and every cell of the pool is checked against its saved page with evidence-check.

Every reason on the recruiting list opens to its source: who to contact first, why, and which approvals apply. Rules fixed before scoring keep the ranking from bending to the list at hand; what public sources lack is written as “not found in public sources” and checked before the first call.

04/ 05

Limits

01“Not found in public sources” does not mean it does not exist, and is no judgement of the merchant

only pages readable without signing in were read. Recommendation: Check the platform’s own seller list before the first call.

02Third-party sales figures are their estimates

the shop ranking comes from one dataset, ordered by units. Recommendation: Re-rank with the platform’s or the merchant’s own data once available.

03No merchant has been contacted and the outreach templates have not been sent

the first-30-days example data is fictional and only tests the SQL. Recommendation: Once outreach starts, record onboarding and week-4 sales, then test the weights with pool/calibrate.py as pool/INSIDE_THE_TEAM.md describes.
