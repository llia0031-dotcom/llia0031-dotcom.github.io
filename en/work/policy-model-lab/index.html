Scenario: judgement

In one minute

To review a short video ad against policy, what must a model see, and can a small model trained on synthetic ads do the job?

On a laptop GPU I ran three preregistered experiments: what a model needs to see of a short video ad (caption, cover, sampled frames, the whole video, text read off the frames) to review it well; whether small models fine-tuned on synthetic ads match a large model; and a check on 300 real short videos from another platform. A survey of the past year’s research closes it.

For video ads the key is letting the model see the words in the picture, not necessarily the whole video: the text read off the frames is enough and much faster. Small models fine-tuned on synthetic ads catch up with the large model on similar items but fall further behind the further they are from the synthetic data; the real-video results are indicative only, since the items and the standard come from another platform.

What I assumed

Expected watching the whole video to be needed; text from frames to fall short

What the data showed

The local 4B model reading only frame text was right on 80/96, as good as watching the video and much faster

  • 64/64 Violations caught with 16 frames (caption only: 25/64) 100.0%, 95% CI 94.3 to 100.0%
  • 80/96 Local model reading frame text only: right 83.3%, 95% CI 74.6 to 89.5%
  • 133/168 Small model tuned on synthetic ads (large model: 138/168) 79.2%, 95% CI 72.4 to 84.6%

Evaluation

  • 16 frames: decisions right (claude-haiku-5-5) 86/96
  • 16 frames: violations caught (claude-haiku-5-5) 64/64

  • Text from 16 frames only: decisions right (Qwen3.5-4B, local) 80/96
  • Whole video: decisions right (Qwen3.5-4B, local) 80/96

Small models fine-tuned on synthetic ads

168 decisions on Grounded Review’s items; 46 real cases; 1 run per model; Qwen3-1.7B with LoRA trained on one laptop GPU; claude-haiku-5-5 through the Message Batches API

  • Grounded Review’s items: right (claude-haiku-5-5) 138/168

Limits

01The video ads and training data are synthetic

the further from them, the more the tuned models drop (mmBERT on real cases: 25/46). Recommendation: Before release, build items from the platform’s own past decisions and test again; retrain with real data where needed.

02The real short videos come from another platform with a different standard, and the two models received frames of different sizes

Recommendation: Re-test on the target platform’s data and standard, with the same input size for every model.

03One run per model per item

some differences fall within the intervals and cannot be separated. Recommendation: Add items and runs for the close comparisons before deciding.
