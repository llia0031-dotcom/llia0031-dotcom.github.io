In one minute

How checkable answers become a review workflow a team runs daily: unsure cases go to people, and prompts ship with A new version first serves a small share of requests, and gets more only if the numbers hold. and Going back to the previous version when the new one misbehaves..

I turned Grounded Review into a four-step review workflow: the model decides, evidence-check verifies the quotes, ads that fail the check or that the model is unsure of go to a human review queue, and reviewed ads come back as A fixed set of questions rerun after every change, to check that what worked still works.; plus a policy retrieval evaluation, a knowledge-base Q&A that answers only from the policy library, prompt versions, canary and rollback rules, weekly reports computed in SQL and a launch checklist, with judging and review workflows built in a Lark Base.

Letting the model say it is unsure reduces wrong decisions that go live at the cost of more human review; the first canary rolled back because the rules themselves were wrong; with fixed rules the second canary on new traffic reached full release and v2 was promoted to production. The evidence that v2 lets fewer errors through still comes from round one. Version 3 cut needless reviews of clear ads but also sent fewer ads that need a human to people; version 4 improved both but was more often sure when wrong. Neither met the standard written beforehand; a canary would not have stopped either, the item design and the adoption standard did.

Limits

01Traffic replays test items rather than real users, and reviewer decisions are the items' fixed answers, not real reviewers

Recommendation: After launch, track wrong decisions let through and review volume per version on real requests, and feed QA samples back as regression items.

02v2 lets fewer wrong decisions through, but human review rose from 6/332 to 72/332, often on clear ads the model called unsure

Recommendation: Version 3 relaxed “unsure” and sent fewer ads that need a human to people; version 4 improved both ends but was more often sure when wrong. The next version has to hold all three: fewer clear ads sent, no fewer ads that need a human, and no wrong decisions let through, again through a regression and new items before a canary.

03Round two reached full release on new traffic, but both versions were almost always right on those ads, so it did not show again that v2 lets fewer errors through

that evidence still comes from round one. Recommendation: Find new ads that v1 misses (longer or vaguer ones) and compare again under the same rules.

04The Lark platform model never said it was unsure (0/25), while the triage model did in 13/50 stored answers

the bot’s Q&A has only been tested, with no real usage. Recommendation: After a model change, measure the review rate again before tuning the unsure threshold; test the bot on a batch of questions, then read its real usage log.

05Retrieval for Chinese questions is weak

the right clause was in the top five for only 23/28, and every “not in the library” on an answerable question came from retrieval missing it. Recommendation: Try embedding or hybrid retrieval for Chinese too, checked first on questions not used to choose the method.
