In one minute

Answer questions from an organisation’s own published rules with word-for-word quotes, which first needs the right page to be found.

We built a desk that answers from published rules with word-for-word quotes: pick a campus, ask, then retrieval, generation and quote checking in code; seven retrieval methods were compared on 45 questions with known right pages, and real student questions were scored separately.

Translating first, then retrieving with reranking works best; real student questions are harder than the written set.

  • 44/45 Written questions: right page in the top five 97.8%, 95% CI 88.4 to 99.6%
  • 14/15 Real student questions: right page in the top five 93.3%, 95% CI 70.2 to 98.8%
  • 19/20 Chinese questions translated first (untranslated: 18/20) 95.0%, 95% CI 76.4 to 99.1%

04/ 05

Limits

01Real wording is harder

MRR 0.724 on students' questions against 0.839 on the written set; the only question missing from the top five was an online exam that lost connection, and the Malay question ranked fifth. Recommendation: After launch, score real user questions the same way as a separate set and keep watching the gap.

02Answer quality itself is not evaluated

in a smoke test one Malaysia-campus question was answered from Australian rules. Recommendation: Add campus-specific items and check that the cited page matches the asker’s campus.
