Scenario: generation

In one minute

When Seedream makes Chinese product images, does the selling-point text come out exactly; how to write and lay it out so the text holds; how to check before listing.

I fixed 20 tasks and froze the rubric before generation, checked the text in every image character by character with OCR and rated each work three times against the rubric; turned the findings into a guide for merchants and built an in-browser pre-listing checker that never uploads the image.

Image text is almost always right and video text does not hold; in appearance 4.5 and 4.0 are close and 5.0 lite is clearly lower.

What I assumed

Expected a large title with top margin to stop titles being clipped

What the data showed

It did not reliably help and titles got smaller; check after generating

  • 16/16 Seedream 4.5 images with every character right 100.0%, 95% CI 80.6 to 100.0%
  • 1/4 Videos with every character right 25.0%, 95% CI 4.6 to 69.9%
  • 80/80 Deliberately wrong characters, all caught 100.0%, 95% CI 95.4 to 100.0%

Evaluation

Main evaluation: 16 image tasks and 4 video tasks

16 images per image version, 4 videos; 1 generation per task; 3 ratings; BytePlus API, free quota

  • Images with exactly right text (Seedream 4.5) 16/16
  • Images with exactly right text (Seedream 4.0) 15/16
  • Images with exactly right text (Seedream 5.0 lite) 16/16
  • Videos with exactly right text (Seedance 1.0 pro) 1/4

Limits

01Small sample

16 image and 4 video tasks, one generation each; only 1/4 videos have exactly right text. Recommendation: Add text to videos in editing rather than relying on generation.

02Ratings were not compared with blind ratings by buyers

all three come from the same rating method. Recommendation: Before launch, have a few real buyers blind-rate a small set and compare with the rubric.

03The prompt mini-experiment has only a few images

stating the text colour worked, asking for a large title with top margin did not reliably help. Recommendation: After generation, check whether the title was clipped and regenerate if so.
