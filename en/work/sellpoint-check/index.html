Sellpoint CheckChecks listing claims against buyer reviews

The listing’s claims are split out, buyer reviews are searched for quotes that support or refute each one, every quote is checked word for word by the evidence-check script, and each claim is labelled supported, refuted, mixed or not mentioned.

Scenario: judgement

In one minute

Which listing claims do buyer reviews confirm: buyers see only confirmed claims with quotes, and the rest go privately to the merchant with suggested rewrites.

I designed claim checking as a feature the platform and merchants share: buyers see only confirmed claims, the rest go privately to the merchant; compared three evidence-finding methods on accuracy and cost; and iterated the merchant advice six times, testing each version on new frozen items before deciding.

Giving the model all reviews in one call is the most accurate and far cheaper than per-review judging; the merchant advice stays at version 5.

What I assumed

Expected hearsay and wishful reviews to be cited as evidence

What the data showed

No trap review was cited, and the cheapest one-call method was the most accurate

  • 60/60 Claims judged right on known answers (one by one: 58/60) 100.0%, 95% CI 94.0 to 100.0%
  • 17% Cost compared with reading reviews one by one
  • 142/151 Rewrite advice merchants can use as is 94.0%, 95% CI 89.1 to 96.8%

Limits

01Test reviews are shorter and cleaner than real ones, and the items were written by the same author after the real-data run

Recommendation: Before launch, repeat the real-data evaluation on the platform’s own licensed reviews.

02Real data has no answer key

agreement between the first and second judgements (C: 88% for supporting and 91% for refuting evidence) shows consistency, not correctness. Recommendation: Sample cases for human review at launch and turn the reviewed cases into new test items.

03The advice grader uses the same model as the generator, and agreement between the two gradings is moderate

Recommendation: Use a model from another provider as a second grader, or grade a sample by hand.

04evidence-check requires Chinese quotes of at least four characters, which blocked two complete three-character review titles

Recommendation: Let the checker accept a short quote when it is the whole review.
