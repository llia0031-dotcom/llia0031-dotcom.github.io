In one minute

Creators fix subtitles, plan shot lists and chase trends weekly. When an assistant does it, do subtitles stay right, can every shot be spoken in time, and do trend facts hold?

I built three Agent Skills for short-video creators: subtitle checking, shot-list checking and trend topics. Each carries a checking script that runs before anything is handed back. Each was compared with the same model without the skill on items whose answers were fixed in advance, with plans and predictions frozen before the runs.

In all three skills the value is in the check: subtitles no longer lose their timings or the speaker’s own words; the shot-list difference only shows when the request gives no rates; trend topics make no marketing cards for events with casualties. The price is a few more model calls; trend topics cost about 2.9× as much as without the skill.

What I assumed

Expected at most half of the shot lists without the skill to pass

What the data showed

With the rates stated almost all passed (19/20); with no rates given none did (0/20)

  • 20/20 Corrected subtitle files fully right (without the skill: 2/20) 100.0%, 95% CI 83.9 to 100.0%
  • 20/20 Shot lists passing with no rates given (without: 0/20) 100.0%, 95% CI 83.9 to 100.0%
  • 4/4 No marketing cards for casualty events (without: 0/4) 100.0%, 95% CI 51.0 to 100.0%

04/ 04

Limits

01All items are synthetic and written with the skills' checks in mind

real audio, accents, music and several speakers are not covered. Recommendation: Before release, test again on real auto-captions and real trends.

02One model and two runs per item per arm

a difference of one or two items is noise. Recommendation: Run the same items again after a model or effort change.

03The shot-list gap appears only when the request gives no rates: with the rates stated, even without the skill 19/20 passed

Recommendation: Look at how users actually ask before deciding whether to install this skill by default.

04Trend topics checks only the sources of facts and casualty events, not the quality of the ideas or their fit to the account

Recommendation: Write separate scoring rules for ideas and account fit, then test.
