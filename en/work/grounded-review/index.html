Scenario: judgement

In one minute

An ad decision should name the rule and the exact words that break it, not just approve or reject.

I extracted 32 policy clauses from official sources, wrote a Questions kept out of all tuning and used only for the final score. with traps, built an agent that looks up clauses, quotes them, revises and re-reviews over several turns, and compared it with a single prompt, a banned-word list and open-source moderation models; iterated the advice from v1 to v1.3; then switched to real ads judged by regulators and courts.

Both arms hit the ceiling on written items; on real ads the agent is stricter than the single prompt, and most errors come from the regulator’s rules differing from the policy library.

What I assumed

Expected misses on small print, small flags and drawn drinking scenes

Limits

01Written items hit the ceiling

both arms are near-perfect on the held-out set and fully right on the harder set, so they cannot separate the agent from the single prompt. Recommendation: To separate them, use items the author did not write: more real rulings, longer videos, or rules the model has not seen.

02The agent rejects more traps

traps approved 55/60 against 59/60 for the single prompt, and it costs more. Recommendation: Add positive examples or clause notes for compliant ads that look like violations (delivery guarantees, heritage listings, set prices).

03Most errors on real ads come from different rules

alcohol ads the ASA cleared lack the alcohol-content line TikTok requires, and both arms rejected them. Recommendation: Keep the policy library identical to the rules being enforced; split it by market.

04Each multi-turn check uses 12 short text ads and one model

a difference of one or two conversations is noise. Recommendation: Before the next advice prompt ships, test it on image ads and longer captions.

05The open-source moderation model takes minutes per image and gives no clause or quote

SafeGuard-VL-RL takes about 178.3 s per image. Recommendation: Use open-source models as a cheap first filter and pass uncertain ads to the grounded review.

06The memory result rests on a single ad, and the short text ads and the store of earlier decisions were written together

Recommendation: Before launch, test on real past decisions and longer ads; keep the memory read-only, with writes confirmed by a person.
